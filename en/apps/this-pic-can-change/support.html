This Pic Can Change · Support

Updated: 22 October 2026.

Designed for iPhone and supports iOS 17 and later. iPad can run the iPhone compatibility layout; there is no dedicated iPad layout. The main tabs, in order, are Workbench, More Features, Recent, and Settings. The interface is Simplified Chinese; translated labels below are paired with Chinese UI names where useful. The app’s Chinese name is 这图能改.

The public TestFlight build 1.2 (5) is open for testing. Visit the product page to join and install it; this version is not on the App Store.

Common places to start

  • Photo and video tools: In Workbench (“工作台”), choose a photo or video and import it. The image editor offers Format (“格式”), Adjust (“调整”), and Crop (“裁剪”); the video editor’s bottom bar switches between Compress (“压缩”), GIF, and Live Photo (“实况”).
  • Screenshot Delivery, AI, and home screen features: Open More Features (“更多功能”) and choose the corresponding entry.
  • AI image generation or editing: Open More Features (“更多功能”), then choose AI Generate (“AI 生图”) or AI Edit (“AI 改图”).
  • Configure an AI service: Open Settings (“设置”) → AI Image (“AI 图片”) → AI Service Configuration (“AI 服务配置”).
  • Purchase or restore: Open Settings (“设置”) → Permanent Unlock (“永久解锁”) or Restore Purchases (“恢复购买”).
  • Privacy information: Open Settings (“设置”) → Privacy (“隐私说明”), or read the privacy policy.
  • Recent results: Open the Recent (“最近”) tab at the bottom.

Photo and video processing

How do I process a file?

Start in Workbench (“工作台”) and import a photo or video. Use Format (“格式”), Adjust (“调整”), or Crop (“裁剪”) for images; the video editor’s bottom bar offers Compress (“压缩”), GIF, and Live Photo (“实况”). Preview the result before saving or sharing. The six core media tools process files on device and do not overwrite the original. More Features (“更多功能”) contains Screenshot Delivery, AI, and home screen features. The app requests permission to add to Photos only when you save a result there. Declining that permission does not prevent processing or use of the system share sheet.

Why did compression miss the target size?

How much a file can be reduced depends on its content. PNG uses lossless encoding and may not meet a target. Keeping the original dimensions can also limit further reduction. Check the actual file size and pixel dimensions shown on the result screen.

Why did HDR photo or video colors change?

The current version does not guarantee preservation of HDR highlights or color. Converting HDR media may change its tone.

Screenshot Delivery

Screenshot Delivery is included in the public TestFlight build 1.2 (5), which is open for testing; this version is not on the App Store. The current feature set and steps are shown in the app.

AI services and charges

How do I configure and check an AI service?

Open Settings (“设置”) → AI Image (“AI 图片”) → AI Service Configuration (“AI 服务配置”), choose a provider, save your own API key, and enter or choose a model. A connection check sends the key to the receiving host shown in the app to fetch a supported model list. It does not send photos or prompts and does not request image generation. ZenMux Gemini Vertex has no model-list request; it only checks the configuration on device. A model appearing in a list does not mean your account can generate or edit images with it.

Why might AI image editing fail?

Image-input support differs by service and model. Before submitting a reference image, check that the provider says the model supports image editing or reference-image input. For a first submission and when you change the service address, protocol, or mode, the app shows the receiving host and content and waits for confirmation. Declining confirmation does not start the task.

Does AI use the app’s weekly free allowance?

No. AI requests use the API key you configure, and charges are set by that provider and account. They are separate from the app’s free allowance and permanent version. A provider may continue processing or billing after a request is submitted, so cancelling the wait may not stop charges. Check the provider, model, account access, and price before submitting.

What should I do if I see “Pending Downloads”?

In More Features (“更多功能”), open the AI task area and choose “Pending Downloads” (“待下载结果”). The AI Generate and AI Edit screens also show a “View and retry unfinished downloads” (“有未完成下载的结果，查看并重试”) shortcut when items are waiting. “Download again” (“重新下载结果”) retrieves an image that was already generated; it does not submit another request. The provider’s temporary link may expire. “Remove download record” (“移除下载记录”) deletes the recovery link and reference image stored on device, after which you cannot retry from this screen.

Where can I clear an AI draft, history item, or API key?

Choose “Clear draft” (“清除草稿”) in AI Generate (“AI 生图”) or AI Edit (“AI 改图”); delete the relevant item in the Recent (“最近”) tab; or clear the current address’s key in Settings (“设置”) → AI Image (“AI 图片”) → AI Service Configuration (“AI 服务配置”). These actions manage local app data. They do not remove copies saved to Photos or shared with another app, or data a provider has retained. Clearing a key does not revoke it with the provider; revoke it in the provider’s account. Do not send API keys, authorization information, private photos, or full prompts to support.

Weekly allowance and permanent version

On-device processing includes 20 successful free uses per week, resetting at 00:00 Monday in the device’s local time. Importing, previewing, failing, and cancelling do not use a count. Lifetime Unlock (“永久解锁”) is a one-time, non-consumable purchase for unlimited use of all on-device tools; it is not a subscription and does not renew. The current App Store price shown in the app applies. Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Home screen widgets

Set up the photo widget in More Features (“更多功能”) → Home Screen Features (“桌面玩法”), choose 1 to 10 photos, and drag thumbnails to reorder them. iOS schedules rotation, so the actual refresh time may vary. Photos fill the widget and may be cropped at the edges; animated images show only their first frame. Four games are also available under Home Screen Features. Photos and progress are stored in the device’s shared app directory and do not use image-processing counts.

Contact support

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, feature name, service host, protocol, model, and error message. Remove keys, tokens, and authorization information first. We usually do not need an API key, original photo or video, or full prompt to begin investigating.
