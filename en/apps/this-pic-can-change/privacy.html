This Pic Can Change · Privacy Policy

Updated: 27 October 2026. Applies to app version 1.2. The app’s Chinese name is 这图能改.

Information we process

The app does not require you to register a developer account. Its seven on-device tools do not upload media to developer servers. Photos and files you select are cached on your device for processing; local Recent items, drafts, and widget data are also stored on the device. Optional AI data flows are described below.

What an optional AI service receives

AI image generation and image editing are optional online features. Choose a feature in Tools (“工具”) → AI Creation (“AI 创作”), then configure a provider, protocol, model, Base URL, and your own API key in Settings (“设置”) → AI Service Configuration (“AI 服务配置”). AI requests go from your device directly to that configured address, not through this app’s developer servers. AI requests do not use the app’s weekly free processing allowance. A provider may charge your account under its own pricing; cancelling your wait does not necessarily stop a request already submitted from being processed or billed.

  • Connection check: When you choose “Check connection and fetch models” (“检查连接并获取模型”), the app sends your API key to the configured address’s model-list endpoint. Protocols that support a model-list check do not send photos or prompts and do not request image generation. ZenMux’s Gemini Vertex protocol only checks the configuration on device and does not request a model list.
  • AI image generation: After you confirm and start, the prompt, selected service and model, and generation options required by that protocol are sent to the receiving service.
  • AI image editing: After you confirm and start, the reference image is first adjusted on device for orientation and format, then sent with your prompt to the receiving service.

Before the first submission for a provider, protocol, full Base URL, and generation or editing mode, the app shows the receiving host and the content to be sent and waits for your confirmation. Confirmation is saved on device for the same configuration and mode. The app has no separate setting to clear accepted-destination records. Confirmation does not start a request; you must still start each AI task yourself. Changing the address, protocol, or mode requires confirmation again. To stop future submissions, do not start another AI task; you can also clear the local API key and revoke it with the provider. A connection check is a separate action, and its button explains where the API key will be sent.

Providers control their own data handling

OpenRouter, ZenMux, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or a custom service address may be operated by different organizations. A receiving service can see the information it needs to process the request and connection details. It may log API requests, prompts, images, account-linked information, network addresses, or generated results. Retention periods, abuse monitoring, model-improvement or training use, deletion methods, and processing locations depend on the provider and your account terms. This app does not control those practices and does not claim that every service promptly deletes requests or never uses them for training. Read the privacy policy and terms of the service you selected. Do not submit prompts or images if you do not accept its handling. You choose who operates a custom Base URL.

Generated-image downloads and local recovery

A service may return an image in its response or provide an HTTPS image link. In the latter case, the app separately downloads the generated image from that link’s host. This download request does not include your AI API key, cookies, or credential store; the link itself may contain temporary access parameters issued by the provider. To support a retry after an interrupted download, the app stores the link, creation details, and, when editing, the processed reference image on your device; these recovery files are excluded from system backups. A link may expire. Retrying from Pending Downloads (“待下载结果”) in Tools only downloads the existing result; it does not submit the generation request again. Removing a pending-download record deletes the local link and reference image but does not ask the provider to delete its copy.

Local drafts, Recent items, and widgets

Separate generation and editing drafts store prompts, style or edit direction, and the processed reference image on device. Restoring a draft does not send an AI request. You can choose “Clear draft” (“清除草稿”) on the corresponding creation page. AI results, prompts, model details, and required reference images are archived in Recent (“最近”) for later viewing and editing. Recent items keep up to 100 entries and up to 1 GB of archived output; older files may be removed when a limit is reached. Deleting a Recent item removes the corresponding app-managed local archive, but does not delete copies saved to Photos, shared with another app, or retained by a provider.

If an AI image was generated but could not be archived in Recent, it may appear in Tools as an Unsaved Image (“未保存图片”) for you to view or remove. The app keeps no more than three unarchived images on device. Images held only in memory are lost when the app exits. Choosing “Remove unsaved image” (“移除未保存图片”) deletes that temporary image and its local record.

Photo widgets and home screen games store your selected photo copies, ordering, and game progress in the local shared App Group directory for the app and widgets to read. These features do not upload photos or progress to developer servers. Ordinary image processing uses temporary caches, which the operating system may manage under its own rules. If processing fails, the app may record the time, processing stage, and system error details locally; details may include a related file path. Error logs are not automatically uploaded to the developer.

The weekly free-use record is stored in the device Keychain. iOS may retain a Keychain item after the app is uninstalled, so the record may remain after reinstalling. Pending-download recovery records, which may contain provider-signed image links, are excluded from system backups. Backup and restoration of other local data depend on system rules and device settings; we do not claim that every local file is excluded from backups.

API keys, purchases, and diagnostics

API keys are stored separately in the device Keychain for each provider and Base URL host, not in ordinary preferences. Keychain access is limited to this device. Connection checks and AI requests send the key to the receiving service currently configured. Clearing a local key does not revoke or delete it with the provider; visit the provider’s account settings to revoke it. Error diagnostics are stored locally and are not automatically uploaded. If you contact support and attach diagnostic information, the support mailbox receives and handles it. Do not email API keys, authorization headers, private photos, or prompts.

Purchases and purchase restoration for the permanent version are handled by Apple StoreKit/App Store. The app reads products and transaction entitlements verified by StoreKit. It does not receive card numbers or payment passwords and does not send purchase records to developer servers. Apple’s handling of store-account and transaction information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current code does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app or cross-site tracking SDKs. Media, drafts, Recent items, and error logs processed or stored on device are not uploaded to developer servers. If you choose to use AI, data is sent to the selected receiving service as described above.

This website and GitHub Pages hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; its analytics are separate from the app. Google Analytics loads only after you explicitly consent and records page views and clicks on TestFlight links; without consent, the Google tag is not loaded and no analytics request is sent to Google. Event parameters are limited to a fixed placement value, page path, and language, without query strings or fragments; external referrers are reduced to their origin and internal referrers to their path. Google Analytics may process browser, device, and visit-request information and set a session-only first-party cookie on the hugehard.app host. Advertising, Google Signals, and ad personalization are disabled. No app media, drafts, prompts, or usage records are sent to Google. Your choice can be changed or withdrawn in “Analytics settings” in the footer. The studio home page and badges page also load badge images hosted by third-party listing sites; those image services receive the related request information. See the badges page for the list. For website details, read the website privacy notice and Google’s Privacy Policy.

GitHub states that it records and retains visitor IP addresses for security when people visit GitHub Pages sites, whether or not they are signed in. Its general privacy statement also says it may record the referring site, visit time, pages viewed, and links clicked. Read the GitHub Pages data collection notice, GitHub General Privacy Statement, and Cloudflare Privacy Policy for hosting and proxy data practices.

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare hosting and proxy logs are website-service data. Google Analytics statistics collected after your consent also cover website visits only; neither is app telemetry and neither receives the app’s on-device media, drafts, or processing history.

Deletion and contact

You can clear generation or editing drafts, delete Recent items, remove pending-download records, and clear the current address’s API key under Settings → AI Service Configuration (“设置”→“AI 服务配置”). These actions manage data on the device in this app. They do not delete copies saved to Photos or shared with another app, and do not delete or revoke data or keys retained by a provider. Follow the provider’s account process for remote deletion or key revocation. To stop future AI requests, do not submit new tasks; you may also clear the local key and revoke it with the provider.
