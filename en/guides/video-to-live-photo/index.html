Video processing

How to turn a video clip into a Live Photo

Written and maintained by HugeHard Studio · Updated 14 October 2026

Choose a short segment, then set its motion range, cover frame, and output quality. This Pic Can Change prepares and creates the paired assets on device; the selected clip can be no longer than three seconds.

Steps

  1. Open Tools (“工具”) → Video to Live Photo (“视频转实况”) and import a clip with the video picker. Videos whose long edge exceeds the 4,096-pixel limit show an unsupported-size notice.
  2. Use the full-video timeline to place the segment’s start and end. The final segment can be at most three seconds; the timeline also enforces a minimum selectable duration. Preview plays the selected part of the original video. Encoding starts only after you tap Generate.
  3. Open the cover-frame panel and choose a still on the timeline inside the selected segment. The cover preview and export use the same precise frame.
  4. Choose Original (“原尺寸”), HD (“高清”), or Compact (“小体积”) quality, then decide whether to keep sound. Original allows a long edge up to 4,096 pixels, HD up to 1,920 pixels, and Compact up to 1,280 pixels. Smaller input is not enlarged.
  5. Check the segment, cover, quality, and sound options, then generate. After previewing the result, save the paired photo and video assets to your Photos library.

Quality and HDR

The three quality settings use different dimension, JPEG-quality, and video-bitrate parameters. They do not promise the same file size or visual result for every clip. HDR tone mapping and HDR metadata preservation have not been implemented or verified on this path; highlights and color in HDR source footage may change.

Review before saving

Confirm that the clip is no longer than three seconds, the cover frame shows the moment you want, and sound is enabled or disabled as intended. If file size or compatibility matters, compare the actual results from the available quality settings. Do not infer a fixed file size or HDR appearance from a quality label.
