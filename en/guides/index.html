Clear steps for common photo and video tasks.

Find steps and limits for photo cropping and rotation, image compression, HEIC-to-JPG conversion, video GIFs, Live Photos, video compression, and Screenshot Delivery.

01How to compress an iPhone photo to 200 KB or another target sizeImage compression

Set a JPEG target and check the actual file size; lossless PNG may miss a target.

02How to crop, rotate, and change a photo’s aspect ratio on iPhonePhoto cropping

Move the selection, rotate the image, set a ratio, and check the result.

03How to turn a video clip into a Live PhotoVideo to Live Photo

Choose a clip up to three seconds, then select a cover, quality, and sound.

04How to join, redact, and deliver screenshotsScreenshot Delivery

This feature is included in the current build; the guide covers joining, redaction, and export checks.

05How to turn a video into a GIF on iPhoneVideo to GIF

Pick a clip, set output size and frame rate, and check the file size.

06How to convert an iPhone HEIC photo to JPGHEIC to JPG

Choose JPEG output, optionally resize or set a target size; the original stays.

07How to compress an iPhone video that is too large to sendVideo compression

Shrink a video on device and check the real size; the original stays.
