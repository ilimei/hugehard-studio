Practical guides · 14 October 2026

Break an image task into clear steps.

These guides follow the current tools and settings in This Pic Can Change, whose Chinese app name is 这图能改. They explain the workflow, output options, and limits. They do not invent test results or promise a particular outcome.

01How to compress an image to a target sizeImage compression

JPEG and HEIC search encoding quality; lossless PNG may miss a target.

02How to turn a video clip into a Live PhotoVideo to Live Photo

Choose a clip up to three seconds, then select a cover, quality, and sound.

03How to join, redact, and deliver screenshotsScreenshot delivery

Choose direction and size, then review OCR candidates, manual redactions, and export conditions.
